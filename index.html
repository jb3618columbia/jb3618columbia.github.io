About

JB

I am a Research Scientist at the Institute of Foundation Models (IFM), where I work on post-training the K2 series of open-weight foundation models. I was a core contributor to K2-Horizon, the latest release in the K2 series.

Before IFM, I was a researcher at Meta. I completed my PhD at Columbia University.
